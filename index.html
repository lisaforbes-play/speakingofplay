About Lisa

Play is the throughline.

I’m a faculty member, licensed counselor, Registered Play Therapist Supervisor, author, and speaker.

My work moves across education, therapy, adult life, organizations, and culture, but the question underneath it is remarkably consistent: what becomes possible when play is allowed back in?

I’m especially interested in what happens when rigid expectations loosen and people have more room to become curious, expressive, connected, experimental, and fully human.
