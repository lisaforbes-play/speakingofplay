About Lisa

Play is the throughline.

I’m a faculty member, licensed counselor, Registered Play Therapist Supervisor, author, and speaker. My work moves across education, therapy, adult life, organizations, and culture—but the question underneath it is remarkably consistent: what becomes possible when play is allowed back in?
