Play across learning, adulthood, organizations, and therapy

Play changes everything.
We’ve just been told it shouldn’t.

Whether we are trying to help people learn, connect, create, grow, or heal, play creates powerful conditions for those things to take place. It makes room for curiosity, expression, courage, connection, and movement.

My work explores play across education, adult life, organizations, culture, and the playroom. These are distinct applications, but they share a belief that people come more fully alive when rigid expectations loosen and play is welcomed back in.

Same word. Different work.

Education

Play rehumanizes learning and invites curiosity, belonging, risk-taking, connection, and meaningful engagement.

Organizations

Play supports creativity, connection, courage, cultural change, and a more human way of working and living.

Play Therapy

Play is the primary language through which children express, process, and make sense of their internal worlds.

The throughline is play. The purpose and application are distinct.

About Lisa

I’m a faculty member at the University of Colorado Denver, a Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC), and a Registered Play Therapist Supervisor (RPT-S™). I co-founded Professors at Play, a global network of faculty exploring playful pedagogy in higher education.

My work lives at the intersection of teaching, therapy, adult life, culture, organizations, and a healthy dose of rebellion. Through keynotes, workshops, trainings, writing, and consultation, I help people and systems create the conditions for greater connection, curiosity, courage, learning, and aliveness.
