For organizations, conferences, leaders, and communities

Play is not just for children. It is one way adults stay human.

I work with organizations, conferences, leaders, and communities to explore how play creates the conditions for creativity, connection, courage, and meaningful change.

This work is not about making everything fun. It is about noticing the norms, habits, pressures, and systems that cause people to become rigid, disconnected, or afraid to experiment—and considering what becomes possible when play is welcomed back in.

What this work explores

The refusal to harden

How small acts of play, delight, imagination, and curiosity help people remain emotionally alive amid stress, grief, responsibility, and pressure.

Creativity and experimentation

How play loosens perfectionism, makes room for risk, and helps people test ideas without needing certainty before they begin.

Culture and social norms

How invisible rules shape behavior, belonging, permission, conformity, and what people believe they are allowed to do.

Connection and psychological safety

How playful conditions can reduce hierarchy, invite authenticity, and create more human ways of being together.

Burnout and aliveness

How organizations can move beyond surface-level wellness and examine the conditions that cause people to become depleted, disconnected, or hardened.

Playful rebellion

How questioning rigid expectations and inherited rules can open possibilities for courage, imagination, and cultural change.

Workshops

Sample topics

Play and the Heart’s Refusal to Harden

Play as a way of resisting emotional hardening under stress, grief, responsibility, and the demands of adulthood.

The Invisible Rules We Live By
